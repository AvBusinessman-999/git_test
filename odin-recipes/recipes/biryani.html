Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

Description

Aromatic spiced basmati rice slow-cooked with marinated chicken tender pieces.

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Marinated Chicken
  • Biryani Masala

Steps

  1. Parboil basmati rice.
  2. Layer cooked rice over chicken in a pot.
  3. Cook on low dum heat for 30 minutes.
