Fresh Mediterranean Salad

Description

A refreshing crisp green salad tossed with garden vegetables and lemon dressing.

Ingredients

  • Cucumbers and Tomatoes
  • Olives and Feta Cheese
  • Olive Oil and Lemon

Steps

  1. Chop fresh vegetables into chunks.
  2. Toss vegetables together in a bowl.
  3. Drizzle dressing and add feta cheese.
